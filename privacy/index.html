Privacy policy

Updated 20 September 2026

Who we are

BibleBubby is a Bible learning app. This policy explains what we collect when you use the app, optional Apple or Google sign-in for progress sync, and subscriptions on the web.

What we collect

Guest learning is stored on this device. When you connect a progress account with Apple or Google, Supabase stores your account identifier, name or email, lesson and daily recall progress, private notes and reflections, saved verses, reading position, and learning preferences so they can be restored. Subscription state is stored so Max can be restored: Apple In-App Purchase on Apple servers, and website checkout through Stripe with a billing identity we keep. If crash reporting is on, Sentry receives diagnostic data such as the crash stack, app version, device type, and performance traces. When you are signed in, those reports include your Supabase account identifier, so crash and performance data are linked to your account; they do not include your email or lesson answers. The App Store build does not send questions or written answers to a language model. Analytics events stay on this device; no analytics endpoint is configured. The app does not serve advertisements. We do not sell personal information.

Payments

US App Store accounts may subscribe to BibleBubby on our website through Stripe. Apple does not process that payment. Stripe sees the card details; we see whether Max is active. Cancel and refund requests for web purchases go to us, not Apple. In other storefronts, Max is an Apple In-App Purchase: Apple processes that payment, and you manage or cancel it in Apple ID settings.

How long we keep it

Deleting the app removes device data but does not delete your synced account data. Account deletion is finished within 30 days of the request. Backups expire with that deletion. Billing records are kept seven years where the law requires.

Your choices

Progress sync is optional. Signing out stops sync but retains progress on this device. Reset progress in Settings to clear the local learning record; deleting the app does not remove cloud copies. Contact support about account and subscription record deletion. Account deletion and subscription cancellation are separate actions.

support@biblebubby.com

Children

BibleBubby is not directed at children under 13. Do not create an account for a child under 13.

Contact

Questions: contact BibleBubby support. Last updated 20 September 2026.
