Support

Updated 18 September 2026

How to reach us

Email BibleBubby support. We use this mailbox for app help, billing questions, and account deletion follow-up.

support@biblebubby.com

In the app

Open You, then Settings, then Help. To delete your account, open You, then Settings, then Delete account. Deletion finishes within 30 days. Backups expire with that deletion. Deleting your account does not cancel Max.

Subscriptions

App Store purchases are managed in Apple ID settings. Website checkout is managed on the BibleBubby website or by emailing support. Apple is not the merchant for website checkout.

Privacy

The privacy policy names email, lesson progress, subscription state, and Sentry crash data. The app does not serve advertisements.
