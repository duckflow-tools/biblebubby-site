Privacy policy

Updated August 22, 2026

Who we are

BibleBubby is a Bible learning app. This policy explains what we collect when you use the app, optional Apple or Google sign-in for progress sync, and subscriptions on the web.

What we collect

Guest learning is stored on this device. When you connect a progress account with Apple or Google, Supabase stores your account identifier, name or email, lesson and daily recall progress, private notes and reflections, saved verses, reading position, and learning preferences so they can be restored. Web subscriptions use a separate Apple-linked billing identity. Stripe processes payment details; we store subscription status. We do not sell personal information.

Payments

US App Store accounts may subscribe to BibleBubby on our website through Stripe. Apple does not process that payment. Stripe sees the card details; we see whether Max is active. Cancel and refund requests for web purchases go to us, not Apple.

How long we keep it

Deleting the app removes device data but does not delete your synced account data. Account data remains until account deletion is completed. Billing records may be retained where required for tax, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.

Your choices

Progress sync is optional. Signing out stops sync but retains progress on this device. Reset progress in Settings to clear the local learning record; deleting the app does not remove cloud copies. Contact support about account and subscription record deletion. Account deletion and subscription cancellation are separate actions.

support@biblebubby.com

Children

BibleBubby is not directed at children under 13. Do not create an account for a child under 13.

Contact

Questions: contact BibleBubby support. Last updated August 22, 2026.
